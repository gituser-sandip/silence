# HyperFrames Hero Motion

## Style Prompt

Old-money editorial still life: warm antique paper, soft rose silk, botanical shadows, and slow museum-like movement. Motion should feel physical and restrained—light drifts across the paper, dust hangs in the air, and petals travel on one quiet air current. No neon, no synthetic glow, no elastic UI motion, and no camera shake.

## Colors

- `#ead3ae` — aged paper base
- `#8b5d3b` — sepia ink and botanical detail
- `#c98c78` — rose silk accent
- `#fff1d1` — warm light
- `#2f241b` — deep brown contrast

## Typography

The motion plate contains no copy; live website typography remains outside the rendered art.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use hard cuts or abrupt zooms.
- Do not duplicate the ribbon as a visibly detached cutout.
- Do not use random or non-deterministic motion.
- Do not add neon, cyan, purple, or glossy 3D effects.
- Do not animate the entire image more than a restrained breathing drift.
